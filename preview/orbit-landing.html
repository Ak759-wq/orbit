About Orbit

A personal academic operating system.

Orbit is built on one idea: your academic life is a connected system, not a pile of apps. Mark attendance and your goals update. Add a test and your analytics move. Plan a session and your free periods get used.

Connected by design

Subjects, schedule, tests, attendance, assignments, sessions and goals share one data model — every module reflects the others instantly.

Honest by default

Every number comes from your own records. No seeded fake scores, no invented averages — if there is no data yet, Orbit says so.

Private and yours

Your records live in your account. Nothing is shared, nothing is sold, and everything exports whenever you want it.
